What Fiction2Reality is

Fiction2Reality (F2R) is a long-term, multi-stage creative venture exploring how fictional and liminal spaces could become places people can actually enter. We are beginning with original digital environments, 3D work, short films, and world-building, with the long-term ambition of creating physical, explorable spaces.

How it began

The idea began with a question: what if a fictional place could be realized as an architecturally-complete environment; not merely a ride, set tour, or decorated room? Early inspirations were Hogwarts from Harry Potter and the Backrooms. Fiction2Reality brings those ambitions together, first digitally, and then eventually as real, explorable places.

The stages

  1. Digital experiments: 3D environments, renders, animations, films, and original world-building.
  2. Feasibility and partnerships: Audience-building, design development, cost analysis, rights, and collaborators.
  3. Physical experiences: Real environments that translate the strongest concepts into places people can explore.
